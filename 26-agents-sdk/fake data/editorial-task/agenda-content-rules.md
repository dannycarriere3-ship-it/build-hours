# Summit 2026 Agenda Rules

- Session titles must be 80 characters or fewer.
- Public abstracts must be 150 words or fewer.
- Speaker bios must be 90 words or fewer and may not publish personal contact information.
- Track and format metadata are required before publication.
- Missing facts must be flagged for review, never invented.
- Generated public copy must preserve the speaker's name and organization as submitted.
- Editorial output should include a reviewer-facing handoff listing changed files and blocked fields.
